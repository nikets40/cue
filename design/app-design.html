2 · Fallback cards for artless sources

Netflix, Prime and Hotstar send title text but no artwork. Instead of a grey music note, the artwork slot gets a bundled brand card — dark, high-quality, per-service — and the Backdrop blur runs off the card's brand hue, so the whole screen still takes on the service's color.

4 · Artwork quality pipeline

MediaRemote hands us one small bitmap (often ~120 px from YouTube Music) with no URL attached. The fix is a three-tier ladder in Booth — the phone always renders the best tier available for the current track.

Tier 1 · Always

MediaRemote bitmap

What Chrome gives macOS. Instant, works for every source, low resolution. Good enough for the blurred backdrop, weak as the crisp cover.

~120–300 px · 0 ms extra
Tier 2 · Music

iTunes Search upgrade

Booth looks up title + artist on the free iTunes Search API and swaps in the 600–1200 px cover. Cached per track; covers nearly all mainstream music on YouTube Music & co.

600–1200 px · ~200 ms, cached
Tier 3 · Phase 2

Extension exact art

The Chrome extension reads the page's own mediaSession.metadata.artwork list and ships the largest size to Booth — source-perfect for every site, including video platforms.

Source native · exact
